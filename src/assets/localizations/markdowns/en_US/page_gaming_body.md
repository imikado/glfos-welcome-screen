Compatible with any x86_64 PC, GLF OS comes with everything you need to game on Linux: Steam, Lutris, Heroic Games Launcher, Faugus launcher, Mangohud preinstalled and configurable using Goverlay.

GLF OS also includes specific configurations to support your hardware, such as your PS5 DualSense™, Xbox™, or 8bitdo® controller, and your racing wheels.
