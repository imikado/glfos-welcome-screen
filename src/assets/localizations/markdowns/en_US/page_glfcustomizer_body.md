GLF OS brings you GLF Customizer, an in-house graphical tool that lets you adapt your system to your needs.

You can pick your edition (mini, standard, studio, studio-pro or streamers), turn software groups or even single applications on or off, switch desktop (GNOME or KDE Plasma) and move from the stable channel (main) to the testing channel.

Every change is reversible. A desktop or channel change takes effect at the next boot.
